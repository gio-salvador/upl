# Name, Mission, and Vision

## The name

The term Synphotodosism comes from the Greek words "Syn," meaning together, "Phos," meaning light, and "Hodos," meaning path or way. Combined, these elements form Synphotodosism, which translates to "the way of coming together in light". This is symbolic of the religion's ethos, which is centred around the unity of all existence manifesting as diverse expressions of divine light and the shared journey towards spiritual enlightenment.

Emerging at a time marked by fragmentation, UPL, the Unified Path of Light, stands as a beacon of unity, understanding, and moral living. With a profound mission rooted in the melding of numerous traditions, UPL lays a harmonious path in the pursuit of divine comprehension and ethical conduct. It envisions a world where love, knowledge, and service guide the actions of all beings, fostering a deeper sense of connection and manifesting the divine light within us.

## The mission

The mission of UPL is to inspire a spiritual journey marked by compassion, wisdom, and enlightenment. It encourages growth and transformation through an unwavering commitment to the principles of unity, ethical living, and continuous learning. It strengthens the spiritual fabric of a global community tied to divine light, encouraging individuals to explore the intricacies of their souls, share profound experiences, and uplift each other on the path of enlightenment.

## The vision

The vision of UPL extends beyond individual growth to collective awakening. It envisions a world where every thought, decision, and action is inspired by a profound sense of interconnectedness and purpose, shaped by divine love, and guided by divine light. By embracing the transformative power of ethical living, positive thinking, and global fellowship, UPL aspires to illuminate the path to a harmonious, enlightened, and compassionate world.
