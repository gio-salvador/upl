# Masaharu Taniguchi and the Power of Positive Thinking

Masaharu Taniguchi, founder of Seicho-No-Ie, is recognised for his contributions to understanding the transformative power of positive thinking and the realisation of the true self as inherently divine. His teachings, which emphasise the oneness of life and the universe, align with UPL's view of the universe as a manifestation of divine light and underscore the importance of aligning one's thoughts and actions with divine principles.
