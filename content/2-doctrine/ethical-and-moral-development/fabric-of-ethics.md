# The Fabric of Ethics

Ethical and moral development is likened to the weaving of a spiritual tapestry, where each thread represents actions, decisions, and intentions aligned with higher principles. This tapestry becomes a reflection of the soul's journey, showcasing its growth, challenges, and achievements in the pursuit of virtue.

The image says three things about an ethical life. No single thread makes the cloth: character is built from many small choices. A thread once woven stays: what is done becomes part of who one is. And a flaw can be worked into the pattern: a wrong that is mended, by making it good and seeking forgiveness, is not the ruin of the cloth but part of its weave.
