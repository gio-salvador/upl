# The Continuum of Life and Consciousness

Death signifies the dissolution of the "material cocoon", but not the termination of life or consciousness. Grounded in the empirically-based proposition called the "hard problem" of consciousness, which questions how physical processes in the brain give rise to subjective experiences, UPL posits that consciousness is not an emergent property of matter but an intrinsic aspect of reality. This view resonates with the concept of dualism, acknowledging the non-material, qualitative aspects of consciousness, separate from the physical brain.
