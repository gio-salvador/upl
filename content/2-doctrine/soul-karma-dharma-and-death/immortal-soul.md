# The Immortal Soul and Its Evolution Beyond Matter

The soul is recognised as an immortal emanation of divine light, transcending the physical form. This view aligns with the idea that humans are not merely matter but spiritual beings on an evolutionary journey. Like the silkworm that crafts a cocoon from silk, humans use the thread of their minds to shape their physical existence. However, this 'body cocoon' is merely a temporary form, a vessel for the soul's experiences and growth in the material world. Upon death, the soul breaks free from this cocoon, emerging into a broader, freer realm to continue its journey. This transition underscores the notion that the essence of man is spiritual, not material.
