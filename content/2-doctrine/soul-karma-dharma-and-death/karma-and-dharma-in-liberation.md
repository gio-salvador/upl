# Integration of Karma and Dharma in the Soul's Liberation

The dynamic interplay between karma and dharma highlights the path of spiritual liberation. By navigating life's challenges with an awareness of karma and a commitment to dharma, individuals refine their 'cocoons,' eventually transcending them. This process of liberation is not an end but a transition to a new phase of the soul's journey, where, freed from the constraints of the physical cocoon, the soul soars into a larger, freer realm of existence.

By liberation UPL means the soul's freedom from what obscures its light. What form the soul's journey takes after death, UPL has not yet defined.
