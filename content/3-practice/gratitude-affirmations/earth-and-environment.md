# Gratitude for Earth and Environmental Blessings

In their daily affirmations, UPL adherents are encouraged to express heartfelt gratitude for the planet and the natural environment that sustains them. This includes gratitude for the beauty and diversity of nature, from the air we breathe, the water that quenches our thirst, the soil that grows our food, and the raw materials that build our homes and societies. Expressing appreciation for the resilience and bounty of the Earth fosters awareness of our ecological footprint and inspires action towards environmental sustainability.
