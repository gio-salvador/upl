# Gratitude for Life's Blessings

Gratitude affirmations extend beyond personal relationships to the various elements of life that provide comfort, safety, and nourishment. This includes being thankful for food on the table, a safe and warm home, clothes to wear, and the convenience brought about by things such as cars and technology. Recognising these blessings nurtures a sense of contentment and appreciation for life's abundance, while also fostering a consciousness about the needs of others and the importance of an equitable distribution of resources.

## An affirmation

> I give thanks for the food on my table, the roof over my head, the clothes I wear and the tools that ease my day. I do not take them for granted. I remember those who go without, and I will share what I have.
