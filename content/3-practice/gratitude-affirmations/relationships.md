# Gratitude for Relationships

In their daily affirmations, UPL followers express gratitude for the people in their lives, including parents, children, partners, business associates, colleagues, friends, family, neighbours, and other members of the community. This practice fosters an acknowledgment of the invaluable contributions each person makes to an individual's spiritual journey. Adherents express appreciation for the love, support, wisdom, and even challenges that these relationships bring, recognising their integral role in shaping personal growth and well-being.
