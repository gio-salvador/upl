# Gratitude for Relationships

In their daily affirmations, UPL followers express gratitude for the people in their lives, including parents, children, partners, business associates, colleagues, friends, family, neighbours, and other members of the community. This practice fosters an acknowledgment of the invaluable contributions each person makes to an individual's spiritual journey. Followers express appreciation for the love, support, wisdom, and even challenges that these relationships bring, recognising their integral role in shaping personal growth and well-being.

## An affirmation

> I give thanks for the people in my life: for those who raised me, those who walk beside me, those I work with and those I meet. I am grateful for their love, their support and their wisdom, and for the difficulties between us, which teach me. May I be to them what they have been to me.
