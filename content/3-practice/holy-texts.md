# Engaging with Holy Texts

Holy texts from various traditions are seen as repositories of spiritual wisdom, each offering unique insights into the nature of the divine, humanity, and the universe. However, UPL expects reading these texts not as literal truths but as symbolic representations and teaching tools. This interpretative freedom allows for a broader understanding of the texts, fostering a more personal, contemplative engagement with the sacred. For UPL, truth transcends the boundaries of any singular holy text, existing instead as a unifying thread woven through all expressions of spiritual wisdom.
