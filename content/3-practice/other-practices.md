# An Open Practice

Spiritual practices serve as a means of deepening connection with the divine, fostering self-awareness, and cultivating ethical living and mindfulness. One defining characteristic of the spiritual practice at UPL is its adaptive, inclusive nature that appreciates the richness of diverse spiritual expressions, allowing its members the flexibility to incorporate elements from various traditions that align with UPL's core values.
