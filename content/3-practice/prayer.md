# Adopting Prayers from Various Traditions

UPL encourages the use of prayers from different religions as a form of spiritual dialogue, offering a means of affirming core beliefs, expressing gratitude, seeking guidance, and fostering a profound sense of connection with the divine. With an understanding that spiritual essence transcends religious boundaries, UPL views these prayers as diverse expressions of a unified spiritual aspiration towards enlightenment and unity.
