# Building a Global Spiritual Network

UPL envisions its community not just as localised gatherings but as a global spiritual network, connected through shared ideals and practices. This global fellowship is facilitated by modern communication technologies, allowing for virtual gatherings, shared meditation sessions, and the dissemination of teachings and experiences across geographical boundaries. This expansive view of community reflects the UPL belief in the universal interconnectedness of all beings and the collective journey towards enlightenment.
