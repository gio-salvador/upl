# Sciences and Technology

## Engagement with Scientific Inquiry

A commitment to understanding the natural world and the principles that govern it. This includes staying informed about advancements in various scientific fields, from physics and biology to environmental science, and recognising that such knowledge deepens our appreciation for the complexity and beauty of creation.

## Technology as a Tool for Good

Technology as a powerful tool that, when used ethically, can enhance human well-being, foster global connections, and support environmental sustainability. Followers are encouraged to engage with technology mindfully, exploring its potential to solve problems, create art, and improve the quality of life for all beings.

As technology matures, guided by ethics, it can help us towards enlightenment and help us to be better human beings: to share knowledge, to improve our medicines and our health, and to raise the quality of life. It has also done harm: industry has damaged the Earth, and industrialised goods have harmed the body. We are learning from these mistakes, and have begun to correct them, as our knowledge of our own impact on the planet and on ourselves improves. UPL holds the hope that through the continuous study and improvement of these fields we may in time transform the Earth into a paradise, a place without misery, hunger or disease.
