# Environmental Stewardship and the Sacredness of Nature

Demonstrate and perform a profound respect for the environment, emphasising the interconnectedness of all life and the sacredness of the natural world. Drawing inspiration from indigenous spiritualities that honour Earth as a living, sacred entity and integrating principles from contemporary ecological movements, UPL advocates for environmental stewardship as an essential expression of spiritual practice. This perspective recognises the Earth as a manifestation of the divine, imbuing every element of nature with intrinsic value and spiritual significance.
