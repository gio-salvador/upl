# The Principle of Interconnectedness

Humans are not separate from nature but integral components of the Earth's ecosystem. This understanding inspires a sense of responsibility towards all forms of life and the ecosystems that sustain them. By recognising the divine presence in all aspects of the natural world, Synphotodosists are called to engage in practices that preserve and honour the Earth.
