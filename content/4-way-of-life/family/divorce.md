# Divorce as a Last Resort

While UPL advocates for resolving conflicts and striving to keep the family unit intact, it acknowledges that in certain situations, the path of divorce may be the most compassionate and sensible route. As a last resort, when all other options of reconciliation have been exhausted, divorce is accepted within the UPL ethos, reflecting respect for individual happiness, freedom, and the journey towards spiritual growth.
