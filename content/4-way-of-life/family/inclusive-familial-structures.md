# Inclusive Familial Structures

A family can exist in many forms; it can be headed by same-sex couples, single parents, or involve multiple generations living under one roof. The formation of family is not bound by gender or singular social constructs, and UPL embraces a broader definition of family, cherishing the bonds of commitment, love, and shared growth that unite its members.
