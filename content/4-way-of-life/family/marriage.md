# Marriage as a Spiritual Union

Marriage plays a treasured role in the UPL community. It is seen as a spiritual union and a commitment, radiating beyond shared domesticity to encompass mutual support, personal growth, and spiritual evolution. Marriage in UPL is open to any two adults, whatever their gender. While not mandated, marriage is encouraged as it establishes a strong foundation for nurturing future generations under the light of UPL teachings and values.
