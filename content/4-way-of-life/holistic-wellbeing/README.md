# Holistic Well-being: Embracing Health, Science, and Sobriety

Prioritise oneself's well-being, physical health, mental clarity, spiritual growth, and ethical living. Central to its philosophy is the promotion of sobriety, a cautious engagement with science and modern medicine, and a clear stance against the use of recreational and illegal drugs. UPL expects its followers to live in harmony with natural laws, making mindful choices that enhance well-being and support spiritual development.
