# Page clarity review

Every page under `content/` read top to bottom on 21 September 2026, at `main` after the matrix
findings closed, and judged on four things: is it clear, is it true to the core beliefs and the
author's decisions, does its argument hold together, and can a newcomer follow it. One line per
page. The plan that fixes what is found here is
[plans/2026-09-21-page-clarity.md](plans/2026-09-21-page-clarity.md), and the "Step" column points
into it. Nothing here changes a teaching: every fix is proposed to the author first.

**Result:** at the time of the review, 48 of 81 pages were good as they stood and 33 needed a fix.
Rows are updated as steps land. Most fixes are one
sentence.

## What recurs

1. **Openings without a subject.** Nine openings on eight pages begin "To envision…", "To value…",
   "Prioritise…", "A commitment to…": headings from the paper that became first sentences when it
   was split. The smallest repair is two or three words each.
2. **Physics.** Decision D1 says the teaching is given "in the language of light, and not as a
   claim of physics". The God page, the Abstract and the Conclusion still make the claim, and one
   statement about the observer effect is wrong as physics.
3. **Tone towards other religions.** Purpose and Comparative Analysis each carry a phrase that
   ranks, against belief 7 and locked decision 6.
4. **Thin pages.** Jesus, Muhammad, the Buddha and Taniguchi get 50 to 74 words and no content of
   their own; Laozi and Guru Nanak get 115 and real teaching. Two more doctrine pages are a single
   image each.
5. **Walls of text.** Four pages hide a good argument in one long paragraph. Breaking a paragraph
   changes no word.
6. **Five names for one person.** Follower (20 uses), adherent (11), member (7), Synphotodosist (3),
   believer. A newcomer cannot tell whether these differ.
7. **Affirmations that are not there.** Four pages describe an affirmation and give none.
